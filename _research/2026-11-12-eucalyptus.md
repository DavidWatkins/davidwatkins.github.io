**Eucalyptus** is a multimodal Universal Manipulation Interface (UMI), accepted as device **D05** for the device exhibition at [The UMI Arena, CoRL 2026 workshop](https://umi-arena.airoa.io/accepted#devices), held on November 12, 2026.

Work with Igor Fala, Zubin Kremer Guha, Amar Hajj-Ahmad, Vidullan Surendran, Tim Fofonoff, Ben Thacher, Richard Burnham, Murphy Wonsick, Zhi Ern Teoh, Ciarán T. O’Neill, and Peter Whitney.
