My advisor chain, traced through the [Mathematics Genealogy Project](https://www.mathgenealogy.org/). Each entry lists the advisor, the institution, and the degree year recorded there. Several people in the chain had two advisors; the list follows the branch that runs through Newton.

1. David Watkins, Columbia University, 2022
2. [Peter Kirby Allen](https://www.mathgenealogy.org/id.php?id=107596), University of Pennsylvania, 1985
3. [Ruzena Kucera Bajcsy](https://www.mathgenealogy.org/id.php?id=39957), Stanford University, 1973
4. [John McCarthy](https://www.mathgenealogy.org/id.php?id=22145), Princeton University, 1951
5. [Donald Clayton Spencer](https://www.mathgenealogy.org/id.php?id=10290), University of Cambridge, 1939
6. [G. H. Hardy](https://www.mathgenealogy.org/id.php?id=17806), University of Cambridge, 1903
7. [Edmund Taylor Whittaker](https://www.mathgenealogy.org/id.php?id=18571), University of Cambridge, 1895
8. [Andrew Russell Forsyth](https://www.mathgenealogy.org/id.php?id=17829), University of Cambridge, 1881
9. [Arthur Cayley](https://www.mathgenealogy.org/id.php?id=7824), University of Cambridge, 1842
10. [William Hopkins](https://www.mathgenealogy.org/id.php?id=42016), University of Cambridge, 1830
11. [Adam Sedgwick](https://www.mathgenealogy.org/id.php?id=102043), University of Cambridge, 1811
12. [Thomas Jones](https://www.mathgenealogy.org/id.php?id=102036), University of Cambridge, 1782
13. [Thomas Postlethwaite](https://www.mathgenealogy.org/id.php?id=133301), University of Cambridge, 1756
14. [Stephen Whisson](https://www.mathgenealogy.org/id.php?id=133367), University of Cambridge, 1742
15. [Walter Taylor](https://www.mathgenealogy.org/id.php?id=133368), University of Cambridge, 1723
16. [Robert Smith](https://www.mathgenealogy.org/id.php?id=103068), University of Cambridge, 1715
17. [Roger Cotes](https://www.mathgenealogy.org/id.php?id=103067), University of Cambridge, 1706
18. [Isaac Newton](https://www.mathgenealogy.org/id.php?id=74313), University of Cambridge, 1668
19. [Isaac Barrow](https://www.mathgenealogy.org/id.php?id=67643), University of Cambridge, 1652
20. [Vincenzo Viviani](https://www.mathgenealogy.org/id.php?id=133302), Università di Pisa, 1642
21. [Galileo Galilei](https://www.mathgenealogy.org/id.php?id=134975), Università di Pisa, 1585
22. [Ostilio Ricci](https://www.mathgenealogy.org/id.php?id=136245), Università di Brescia
23. [Niccolò Fontana Tartaglia](https://www.mathgenealogy.org/id.php?id=136514), d. 1557

The Genealogy Project notes that Barrow was not Newton's formal advisor (Benjamin Pulleyn was his tutor) and that Barrow learned mathematics from Viviani in Florence after his Cambridge degree. Cayley's year is his Cambridge B.A.; the Genealogy Project records only his later honorary doctorates.
