**Eucalyptus** is a multimodal Universal Manipulation Interface (UMI), accepted as device **D05** for the device exhibition at **The UMI Arena: From Handheld Devices to Real-World Policies**, a CoRL workshop held on November 12, 2026.

**Authors:** Igor Fala, Zubin Kremer Guha, Amar Hajj-Ahmad, Vidullan Surendran, Tim Fofonoff, Ben Thacher, Richard Burnham, Murphy Wonsick, Zhi Ern Teoh, Ciarán T. O’Neill, Peter Whitney, and David Watkins.
