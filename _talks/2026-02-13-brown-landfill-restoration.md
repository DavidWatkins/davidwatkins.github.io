Guest lecture in **Stefanie Tellex's** class at **Brown University** on robotic manipulation for landfill restoration.
The talk covered multimodal sensing (force, audio, spectroscopic), the capability gap between warehouse robots and unstructured-environment robots, and why landfill sorting may be the hardest data collection problem in robotics.

Thanks to Stefanie for the invitation!
