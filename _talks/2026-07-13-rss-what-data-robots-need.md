Invited talk at the [It's the Demos workshop](https://its-the-demos.github.io/) (A Deep Look at the Role of Demonstration Quality in Imitation-Based Robot Manipulation) at RSS 2026 in Sydney, Australia.

The talk traces the arc of robot data collection across my work at the **RAI Institute** and at **Data Factory 1 (DF1)**, Tutor Intelligence's 100-robot fleet: from co-designed handheld force-based capture for in-the-wild demonstrations to the fleet-scale data flywheels that keep demonstration quality high across a large, internationally distributed operator pool.
