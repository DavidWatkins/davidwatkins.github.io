Co-organizing the [Tactile Sensing for Robotic Foundation Models](https://tac-for-fm.github.io/rss2026/) workshop at RSS 2026 in Sydney, Australia. The workshop explores what role tactile sensing should play in the emerging era of robotic foundation models and large-scale data collection.
